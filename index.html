Call Scripts

Your framework for every call. Read before you dial.

Call 1 — Discovery Framework
1

Pre-Call Research

Before the call, find out what they've said publicly about their situation. Social media, comments, posts in the TMMB community. Come in with one specific observation so they know you've done your homework.

2

Opening — Put Them at Ease

This is not a sales call in their mind yet. Keep it conversational. Find out where they are right now before you do anything else.

"Hey [name], good to speak. So I've got a bit of context from the form you filled in — but I'd rather just hear it from you. Where are you at right now with TikTok Shop?"
3

Diagnose — Surface the Gap

Let them talk. You're listening for the gap between where they are and where they want to be. Probe on the emotional side, not just the practical.

"And what's the dream with this? Like if TikTok Shop is working properly for you in 6 months — what does that actually look like for your life?"
4

Amplify the Pain

Get them to articulate what staying where they are actually costs them. Not just money — time, opportunity, the life they're not living.

"So if nothing changes in the next 6 months — what does that look like for you practically?"
5

Give Value Before You Leave

Drop something specific and useful based on what they've told you. A product angle, a content tip, something that proves you know the space. This is the moment that builds trust.

6

BAMFAM — Book a Meeting From a Meeting

Never leave without a booked call 2. Get them to commit to the next step before they hang up.

"Right, based on everything you've told me I think there's a real fit here. What I want to do is get you on a call with [Tom/Aaron] to go through the full Academy and see if it makes sense for you. Are you around Thursday or Friday?"
Call 2 — Close Framework
1

Recap Their Pain in Their Words

Open by reflecting back what they told you on call 1. Use their exact language. This shows you listened and re-anchors them to why they booked the second call.

"So when we last spoke, you said [their words]. Has anything changed since then, or is that still where you're at?"
2

Present the Solution

Walk through the Academy specifically as the answer to the gap they described. Not a generic pitch — connect each element to what they told you they need.

3

Case Studies — Make It Real

Drop Caleb or Kelly at the right moment. Pick the one closest to their situation. Don't recite it — weave it in naturally.

"We had someone in a really similar position — [Caleb/Kelly] — [their situation]. Within [timeframe], they were doing [result]. That's what this looks like when it works."
4

Handle Objections — Then Ask for the Decision

When they raise something, address it directly and return to the question. Never let an objection become the end of the conversation.

5

The Close

"Based on everything we've talked about — this clearly makes sense for where you're trying to get to. What's stopping you from getting started today?"

Then shut up. Whoever speaks next loses. Let the silence do the work.

Opening Lines — Use These
"Hey [name], how are you getting on? So before I go into anything — where are you at right now? What made you book this call?"
"Good to meet you. I've had a quick look at your profile — [specific observation]. Tell me more about where you're at."
"So you've been in the TMMB community for a bit — what's been the main sticking point for you so far?"
What NOT to Open With
  • Don't open with the pitch — ever
  • Don't ask "so what do you know about the Academy?" — it sounds scripted
  • Don't jump to price before you've surfaced the gap
  • Don't talk more than them in the first 5 minutes
Diagnostic Questions — Discovery
  • "Where are you at right now with TikTok Shop — have you started, or still exploring?"
  • "What's working, and what's feeling like the sticking point?"
  • "What does your content output look like right now — how many videos a week?"
  • "Have you found any products that have converted well, or is that still the challenge?"
  • "What's the dream with this — if it's working in 6 months, what does that look like for you?"
  • "Is there a number that would change things for you? Like a monthly income that would actually shift your situation?"
  • "What's held you back from getting there so far — is it knowledge, time, knowing what products to focus on?"
  • "If nothing changes in the next 6 months, what does that actually cost you?"
B2C Mindset Shift

These buyers are investing in themselves, not their business. The hesitation is almost never logical — it's fear, self-doubt, or past failure. Your job is to find the emotional blocker and remove it. Logic closes B2B. Emotion closes B2C.

Close Sequence
1

Trial Close — Test the Temperature

"Does this feel like it could be the right move for you?"
2

The Assumptive Close

"So in terms of getting started — are you looking at getting in straight away, or is there something specific we need to work through first?"
3

The Direct Close

"Based on everything you've told me — this is clearly the right move. What's stopping you from getting started today?"

SHUT UP AFTER THIS. Do not fill the silence.

4

After a "Think About It"

"What specifically do you need to think about? Let's think through it now — I'd rather you make a decision you're confident in on this call than leave with uncertainty."

Objection Bank

Every objection you'll face and exactly how to handle it.

Fear Objections — Most Common at £4k B2C
Fear
"I need to think about it."
"What specifically do you need to think about? Let's think through it now — I'd rather you make a decision you're confident in on this call than leave with uncertainty." Then shut up.
Fear
"I'm not sure I'll follow through / make it work."
"That's actually the most honest thing anyone says on these calls. So let me ask — what would have to be true for you to make it work? Because that's exactly what the Academy is built around. You're not doing this alone."
Fear
"I've tried things like this before and it didn't work."
"What happened? [Listen fully.] Right — and that's exactly why what we do is different. [Specific differentiator based on what they said.] The people who've been burned before are actually our best members because they know what doesn't work."
Fear
"What if I don't make my money back?"
"Kelly was a nurse making £200 a month when she started. Caleb was at £4k. The question isn't whether it works — the question is whether you're willing to put the work in. What would your situation look like if you made even half of what they did in month one?"
Logical / Practical Objections
Logic
"£4,000 is a lot of money."
"It is. And Kelly made her whole nursing salary in January — her first full month after going all in. What does £4k look like against a month of that? The question isn't whether it costs £4k. It's what it costs you to stay where you are."
Logic
"I don't have the time."
"Kelly was doing night shifts as a nurse and still found time to build this. What she got from the Academy was a clear system so she wasn't wasting the time she did have. How much time are you currently spending on TikTok without a clear direction?"
Logic
"Can I not just figure it out myself?"
"You can. But Caleb was figuring it out himself for months before the Academy and plateaued at £4k. Kelly said without TMMB she would have quit entirely. The difference is not the information — it's the product access, the community, and having people who can tell you exactly which products to go hard on."
Value / Scepticism Objections
Value

"The information is out there, yeah. What you can't find for free is the brand retainer deals, the direct product recommendations from Tom, and 20 other creators in the same room who are hitting £10k to £20k months and will tell you exactly what's working right now."
